A paper copy: no money is involved. General information only, not financial advice. Copy trading can lose money quickly. Past results don't predict future ones. Not affiliated with Strike Finance.

How it works: each time this page opens, it replays the wallet's real trades on Strike markets since the start date with these settings, Strike's base fee (0.05% per side), closes 10 bp worse, hourly prices and funding at Strike's own hourly rates. The trial stores only the wallet and the settings, and ends after 30 days.

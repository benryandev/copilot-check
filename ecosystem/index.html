Drive around Strike

Follow the dirt roads and signposts from Strike HQ to each district. Pull onto a glowing pad to open that feature, with live figures from Strike where they exist.

  • ↑ W accelerate
  • ↓ S brake, reverse
  • ← → A D steer
  • Enter open a pad
  • M map view

On a phone, use the buttons at the bottom of the screen. Looking for something? Map view jumps you straight there. Race a lap puts you on the RealFi Raceway start line, with a live top 10. The Fill Yard, off the road to Perpetuals, shows every trade on Strike as it happens. Other visitors can see your car and the name you chose. Built with three.js; the code is on GitHub.

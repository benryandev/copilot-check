Builders' Row

List your project on the Strike map

Building on Strike? Apps, bots, dashboards and tools that use Strike's API, builder codes or vaults can have a shop on Builders' Row, in the Developers district of the drivable Strike ecosystem map.

What gets checked

  • It's live and works.
  • It really uses Strike.
  • It isn't posing as someone else.
  • Its claims hold up.
  • It never asks for a seed phrase, and doesn't hold people's funds without saying so.

What happens next

Ben Ryan reads every submission. If it passes, your shop goes up with your name and logo, and its sheet links your site. Listings are free. To change a listing later, send this form again and pick "Update a listing".

This map is independent and not affiliated with Strike Finance. A listing isn't an endorsement by Strike or by Ben.
